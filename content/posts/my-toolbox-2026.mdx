In Minecraft your hotbar is nine slots, and what you keep in them says everything about how you play. Here's mine for 2026 — the tools that earn a slot by being reached for daily.

## Slot 1–3: the pickaxes

- **TypeScript** — the diamond pickaxe. Types are the torches you place on the way down; future-you sees the cave.
- **C++** — the iron pickaxe that never breaks. Competitive programming keeps it sharp; `#include <bits/stdc++.h>` is a lifestyle.
- **Python** — the shovel. Not glamorous, moves absurd amounts of dirt: scripts, data wrangling, model experiments.

## Slot 4–6: the utility items

- **Next.js + Three.js** — the workbench where this site got crafted. Server components for the heavy lifting, a physics engine when the page needs gravity.
- **Git** — the respawn anchor. Commit early, and no explosion is permanent.
- **An AI pair programmer** — like a villager with suspiciously good trades: you still choose what to build, but the emeralds go further.

## Slot 7–9: the consumables

- **A paper notebook** — the crafting recipe I keep rediscovering. Drawing a box around a problem beats staring at it.
- **Coffee** — splash potion of swiftness. Diminishing returns after the second.
- **An empty slot** — on purpose. Room for whatever this year drops.

---

Tools change; the criterion doesn't. If I stop reaching for it, it leaves the hotbar and goes in a chest. No sentimentality — chests are for sentimentality.
